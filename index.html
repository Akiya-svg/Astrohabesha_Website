Who We Are

Empowering Next-Gen Astronomers

AstroHabesha is a premier student-led organization in Ethiopia dedicated to making astronomy and space science accessible, exciting, and actionable for young minds across the nation.

Through observations, workshops, and innovative projects, we cultivate curiosity and build technical capabilities in aerospace fields.

Our Mission

"To inspire, educate, and elevate Ethiopian youths by fostering a collaborative community passionate about space exploration, stargazing, and scientific literacy."

Active Data Stream

Exoplanet Transit Analysis

Learn to detect and analyze alien world configurations orbiting distant star systems. By mapping photometric light curves using Python scripts, students track the microscopic drops in ambient starlight caused when a celestial body crosses its host star's boundary path.

Scientific Impact Notes: This pipeline uses real photometric target files matching transiting data sets from the Kepler and TESS missions to help classify exoplanet radii and orbital periods.

Citizen Science

Asteroid Hunting Campaigns

Track over thousands of minor planetary objects and Near-Earth Objects (NEOs). In global coordination with the International Astronomical Search Collaboration (IASC), our members process raw, high-resolution sky survey images to isolate moving targets from background coordinates.

Scientific Impact Notes: Volunteers use Astrometrica to perform precise astrometric data measurements, contributing directly to the discovery, validation, and naming of real solar system bodies.
Track Closest Asteroids →
Imaging Lab

Astrophotography Processing

Peel back cosmic dust layers and render deep-space stellar nurseries visible. Students master professional software suites to process flexible image transport system (FITS) data files, learning how to layer separate data wavelengths into single true-color configurations.

Scientific Impact Notes: Utilizing open-source utilities like FITS Liberator and GIMP, participants convert scientific satellite data exposures into high-definition deep sky maps of nebulae and galaxies.
View Cosmic Maps →
Symposia Matrix

Global Scientific Assemblies

Bridge the gap between student researchers and international astrophysics boards. We connect aspiring young scientists in Ethiopia directly with experienced researchers, astronomical societies, and space education pioneers from around the globe.

Scientific Impact Notes: Facilitates research cooperation, career mentorship pathways, and live interactive technical knowledge exchanges with professional astronomy institutions.
